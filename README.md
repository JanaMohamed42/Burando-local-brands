## 🎯 Primary Features: We Must Have

### 1. From the User's Perspective

#### 1.1 Onboarding
Users are introduced to the application's core functionalities through a series of simple screens. Afterward, they can select their preferred categories of interest.

#### 1.2 Sign-Up and Login
Users can create an account by providing basic information such as name, email, phone number, and password with confirmation. The login feature allows existing users to access their accounts easily.

- Enable **visit as a guest** options without signup & login

#### 1.3 User Home Page

- **Product Discovery:** Users can browse products through categories and a main feed for visitors based on random or top-rated
- **Search bar:** A simple text-based search for products or stores
- **Advanced Filtering:** Allows users to refine search results based on Category, Price Range, Customer Rating
- **Categories Sidebar** (e.g., clothing, food, accessories)
- **"Recommended for You" section:** Powered by smart recommendations based on liked, saved, bought, followed or featured items or brands

#### 1.4 Product Details
Every product can be opened in a new page viewing its full details.

#### 1.5 Product Recommendation
Under each product being explored from product details, there will be similar products suggested to the user from the same brand or other brands that user may like.

#### 1.6 Order Placement & Shopping Cart
Users can place orders and view the items they have added to their cart.

#### 1.7 Checkout
A multi-step process to confirm address and place the order.

#### 1.8 Payment Gateways
Pay with Cash or Vodafone Cash / InstaPay (Cashless transactions preferred). Profits/commission will be from brand owners.

#### 1.9 Simple Order Tracking Status
Users can track the status of their orders, which may display as Pending, Processing (Accepted or Rejected), In Preparing, or In Delivery.

#### 1.10 Order Resolution & Feedback Center
This is a single, comprehensive feature accessible from the user's order history page. It serves as a central hub for all post-purchase actions.

**Components:**
- **Write a Product Review:** Allows the user to give a star rating and write their opinion
- **Leave Delivery Feedback:** Allows the user to rate the delivery experience (speed, packaging quality, courier professionalism)
- **Request a Return or Replacement:** Integrated system to formally request a return or replacement for an item

#### 1.11 Brand & Product Ratings & Review
Allows customers to provide ratings/reviews for brands/products.

#### 1.12 Customer Profile & Account Page Settings
- Edit personal details (name, email, password)
- Manage shipping addresses
- View order history
- Log out

#### 1.13 Order Page
Order history with delivery status.

#### 1.14 Push Notifications
Alerts for new offers, order updates, or direct messages.

#### 1.15 Wishlist
Lets users save products they are interested in for later.

#### 1.16 App Settings
- Manage notification preferences
- Manage privacy options
- "About Us" page
- (Future) Change app language

---

### 2. From the Local Brand Owner's Perspective

#### 2.1 Onboarding
Brand owners are introduced to the application and its functionalities. They select their brand's category, specify the brand name, upload a logo, and indicate the business's location (governorate). The system then builds their store.

#### 2.2 Brand Owner Registration
Brand owners register by providing basic details such as name, email, phone number, and password. Adding a bank account is optional.

#### 2.3 Brand Profile Page
- Enables brand owners to create and set up their store profile
- Content: Brand name and logo, brief description, list of products with images, customer ratings and latest reviews

#### 2.4 Home Page
Brand owners can monitor their brand's latest updates, view products from other brands, and see comments and reviews on products, dashboards & explore just like users do.

#### 2.5 Product Management Section
Brand owners can add, edit, or delete their product listings.

#### 2.6 Order Management
Brand owners can view new orders and update their fulfillment status and send a receipt containing all transaction info to user's email address after paying.

#### 2.7 Order Simple Notifications System
Brand owners receive continuous notifications when a user places an order for a specific product.

#### 2.8 Settings Section
- Manage privacy options
- Notification window to view messages from brand owners
- About us
- Change language
- Log out

---

### 3. From the Super Admin's Perspective

#### 3.1 Admin Dashboard
The super admin monitors all registered users and brand owners, their chosen categories, classifications, and ratings through a comprehensive dashboard.

## ✨ Secondary Features: Nice to Have

### 1. From the User's Perspective

- **AI Recommendations through Chatbot:** Suggests relevant products based on user behavior
- **Real-Time In-App Chat:** Direct messaging channel between buyers and sellers
- **Follow & Interact with Brand:** Allows users to follow and interact with favorite brands
- **Points System:** Loyalty program rewarding users with points for purchases or popular reviews/comments
- **Top-Rated Sellers Section:** On each category section

### 2. From the Local Brand Owner's Perspective

- **Shorts Section:** Upload short promotional videos to showcase products
- **Stories for Brand Owners:** Post stories for products or new stuff to notify followers or customers
- **Order Tracking Status Updating:** Track order statuses with shipping companies and update users directly
- **Advanced Notifications:** Receive notifications about reviews, orders, likes, comments, and new followers
- **Promo Code Generator:** Create custom discount codes to drive sales
- **Stock Management Notifications:** Log products and quantities, receive alerts when stock is low
- **Brand Owner Analytics:** Insights on profits, top-selling products, most active governorates, and products with highest positive or negative comments

### 3. From the Super Admin's Perspective

- **Premium Plans for Services**
- **Support Chatbot:** Automated bot to answer common user questions 24/7

---

## 🔮 Future Scope

- **Dynamic Offer Banners:** Show users the latest offers of brands
- **Custom Product Options:** Provide tools, templates & mockups for custom-made orders
- **In-App Ads:** Purchase advertising space within the application
- **Integration with Local Delivery Companies**
- **"Featured" Section:** For active or sponsored brands
- **Adaptive Profile Styling:** Brand owners can customize their profile layout
